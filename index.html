01 — THE STORY

The Man Behind
The Groove

James Earl Lofton — better known as J Lofton — is one of Chicago's hottest DJs, spinning since 1983. Producer, songwriter, remixer, and a bona fide House Music Pioneer.

He's the original founder of 2 House People, formed in 1987 and known today as 2Housspeople. Their debut "Move My Body" dropped on the legendary Trax Records, with follow-ups on Neco Records, Olio Entertainment, and Gherkin Records.

His production "Baby Wants To Move You" — with pioneer Brett Wilcott — was written up in a dance-music magazine as one of the best hip-house records of the '90s. Now CEO of 2HoussMusic and based in Phoenix, Arizona, J Lofton keeps creating new House, Electronica & Dance.
